My Story

I build AI infrastructure for people the industry skips. A BIT student in Kathmandu, I ship commercial products on the side — work that doesn't wait for a diploma to matter.

My flagship build, Nepal AI Gateway, is a commercial LLM proxy built from scratch: NPR-tiered pricing through eSewa, for Nepali developers locked out of international payment rails. Underneath it — a Cloudflare Worker architecture, a full security audit that closed real vulnerabilities, and an admin console I designed myself.

At The Mindsnack, an official distribution partner of Springbase AI, I run AEO and SEO strategy and redesigned the company's own site from the ground up, based out of The 100 Spaces coworking space in Kathmandu.

The plan is deliberate: Nepal's AI companies first, remote AI roles next, an AI lab after that. I'm rebuilding my fundamentals through CS50P and contributing to open source along the way — no step skipped.

Before AI was the job, it was NEPSE trading operations across multiple Demat/TMS accounts and HikCentral CCTV administration — the unglamorous machinery of running an office. I still call myself an office executive who leverages AI, not a developer. The rest, you'd have to find out by working with me.
