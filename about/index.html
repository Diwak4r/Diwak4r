My Story

I'm a BIT student at Himalayan Whitehouse International College — and a full-time Executive Assistant at MC Group of Companies, Kathmandu. I leave home at 6:50 AM, college till 10, office till 8 PM. Repeat five days a week. Somewhere between that schedule and exams, I try to build things that matter.

My job is technically "Executive Assistant" but practically it's IT guy + share market ops + document wrangler. I manage 15+ Demat accounts, troubleshoot network drops before breakfast, and generate reports that actually help Directors make decisions. The Excel skills aren't from a course — they're from real deadlines.

I'm not where I want to be yet. My C programming is near zero, DSA is something I'm still fighting through, and I know the gap between "uses AI tools" and "builds AI systems" is a canyon, not a step. But I'm putting in the work — slowly, consistently, without waiting for motivation to show up.

This portfolio is part of that work. It's me showing up, building in public, and being honest about the climb.
