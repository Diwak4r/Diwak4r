Privacy and cookies

Last updated 4 November 2026

What I count without asking

Cloudflare Web Analytics counts page views and how fast pages load. It sets no cookies and stores nothing that can identify you.

What I count if you say yes

If you accept analytics, this site loads Google Analytics. It sets two cookies, _ga and one that starts with _ga_. They last up to two years.

It records the pages you open, how far you scroll in a blog post, the links you click, your device, browser and operating system, your country and city, and whether you have visited before. It also records which page led you to the newsletter form.

Google shortens your network address and does not keep the full one. I turned off Google signals and ad personalization. I do not use this data for ads, and I do not sell it.

If you say no, or change your mind

If you say no, nothing from Google loads. You can change your choice at any time with Cookie settings in the footer. If you choose no, the Google cookies are deleted from this browser.

Newsletter

If you subscribe, I store your name, your email, the page you signed up from, and the date. I also store a scrambled form of your network address. It limits spam and I cannot turn it back into your address.

I use this only to email you about new posts. Every email has an unsubscribe link. To have your data deleted, write to me from the contact page.

Visits from links

When you arrive from another site, or from a link with a campaign tag, I count one visit. I keep the campaign name or the name of the site that sent you, the page, and the day. I add it to a total.

I store no cookie, no network address, and nothing that points to you. This count runs whatever you chose about analytics cookies.

Contact form

Your message goes through formsubmit.co to my inbox. I answer it, and I do not add you to the newsletter.

Who holds the data

Cloudflare runs this site's signup form and keeps the subscriber list. Google holds analytics data if you accept it. FormSubmit delivers contact messages.
