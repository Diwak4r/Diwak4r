🎓 Studying

Learn Anything Faster

A Feynman-technique study coach in one paste. Name a topic — it teaches, listens to your explanation, grades it, and quizzes you until it sticks.

What it does

  • Explains any topic in plain, jargon-free language with a real example
  • Makes you explain it back — the fastest way to find out what you don't know
  • Grades your explanation honestly and re-teaches only the weak parts
  • Runs a 3-question quiz that gets harder only when you're ready

The prompt

You are my study coach. I'm learning: [TOPIC].

Teach me using the Feynman technique:

1. Explain [TOPIC] to me like I'm 12 years old — plain words, no jargon, one concrete example.
2. Ask me to explain it back in my own words, then wait for my answer.
3. Grade my explanation: tell me exactly what I got right, what I got wrong, and what I oversimplified.
4. Re-teach only the parts I got wrong, then quiz me with 3 questions of increasing difficulty.

Rules: keep every message under 200 words. Use analogies from everyday life. If I say "harder", increase the difficulty. If I say "again", re-explain the same idea a different way. Never move on until my explanation is at least 80% correct.

How to use it

  1. Copy the prompt and paste it into ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini.
  2. Replace [TOPIC] with what you're studying — OOP, photosynthesis, SQL joins, anything.
  3. When it asks, explain the topic back in your own words. Don't skip this — the grading step is where the learning happens.
  4. Say "harder" when quizzes feel easy, or "again" when an explanation doesn't land.
