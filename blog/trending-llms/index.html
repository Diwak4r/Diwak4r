Best Free AI Tools, LLMs, and Agent Platforms in 2026

Reviewed in March 2026. 18 AI tools worth testing with free access or generous free tiers.

Updated April 2026. added Zo agentic platform. Tool names, free plans, and limits move fast. Double-check the official site before you depend on any single feature.

📅 February 2026 ⏱️ 5 min read ✍️ Diwakar Ray Yadav

Look, new AI tools drop every week. Most are repackaged wrappers. Some are straight up vaporware. But a few? A few are genuinely insane. and completely free to use.

I spent the last few weeks going through way too many of these. This is the shortlist that survived. Each one earned its spot because I either use it regularly or it impressed me enough to bookmark it on the first try.

No rankings. No affiliate nonsense. Just click and try.

🤖 Agents That Actually Do Stuff

Give them a task. Walk away. Come back to results. That's the promise. and these ones actually deliver.

General Agent
Manus AI
A general-purpose agent for research, planning, and multi-step tasks. Best when you want the tool to break the job into steps and work through it for you.
manus.im/app →
Task Executor • Claude 4.6
Claude
Claude 4.6 is still one of the strongest options for writing, reasoning, and long-form editing. Free access exists, but limits vary based on demand.
claude.ai/new →
Long Context • Agent Workflows
Kimi
A good option for long-context chat, document-heavy tasks, and multi-step research workflows. Useful when you want fast setup and a lot of built-in tools.
kimi.com →
Agent
Minimax Agent
Surprisingly good at multi-step tasks. Throw something complex at it. See what happens.
agent.minimax.io →
Agent
Zo
A newer agentic platform worth keeping an eye on. Built for task automation and multi-step workflows. early but promising.
zo.computer →

📊 Data, Sheets, Slides. Without the Pain

You know that feeling when you're staring at a spreadsheet and questioning your life choices? These fix that.

Data Analyst
Julius AI
Drop a CSV. Ask a question in plain English. Get a chart. That's it. That's the whole thing. It's beautiful.
julius.ai →
Excel Agent
Shortcut AI
An AI that lives inside your Excel workflow. Formulas, cleaning, analysis. never write VLOOKUP again.
shortcut.ai →
Slides • Data • Reports
GLM-5 (Z.AI)
Good for slide decks, reports, and general assistant tasks. Worth checking when you want a free all-rounder with productivity-focused output.
chat.z.ai →
Chat • Image • Voice • Reasoning
ChatGPT (OpenAI)
GPT-5-class reasoning, image and voice input, plus a generous free tier. Still the default for most people.
openai.com →

🧪 The Reasoning Layer

For when you need actual reasoning, not just vibes. OCR, image understanding, deep thinking, fast second opinions.

OCR • Multimodal • Gemini 2.5
AI Studio (Google)
One of the best free places to test multimodal Gemini models, especially for OCR, documents, and image-heavy tasks. Great when you want raw model access without too much product friction.
aistudio.google.com →
Gemini 2.5 • Everyday Assistant
Gemini
A strong everyday assistant for search-heavy questions, multimodal input, and quick drafting. Paid plans unlock more, but the base app is still useful for free.
gemini.google.com/app →
Reasoning • Chat
DeepSeek
A useful reasoning-first chat option with free public access on web and mobile. Good when you want a fast second opinion next to Claude or Gemini.
chat.deepseek.com →
Model Router • API
OpenRouter
One API key for dozens of models. Useful for comparing responses and pricing without juggling multiple accounts.
openrouter.ai →
Web Search • Multimodal
Grok 4
Strong for live web-heavy questions and image-aware tasks. Access levels and rate limits change often, so check the current plan before you rely on it for daily work.
grok.com →
Open Weight • Multimodal
Llama 4 (Scout & Maverick)
Scout (109B) and Maverick (400B). natively multimodal, open-weight models. Try via Meta AI, Llama API, or partner platforms like AWS Bedrock.
llama.com →
Deep Research • Prediction
MiroThinker (MiroMind)
An open-source deep-research style agent focused on multi-step browsing, synthesis, and code execution. Worth a look if you want a more research-oriented workflow.
dr.miromind.ai →

🖥️ Everything Else

AI that takes over your screen, and a place that lets you try literally everything.

Agent-Native Computer
HappyCapy AI
Agent-native computer for developers. Works with Claude Code, 150+ AI models, secure sandbox. Let AI agents work for you 24/7.
happycapy.ai/app →
Images • Text • Code • Video
Arena
Battle Mode: compare models side by side. ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini. all free, crowdsourced benchmarks and leaderboards.
arena.ai →

Honestly?

Most of these free tiers will cover 90% of what you need. My current setup: ChatGPT / Claude for writing and reasoning, Gemini for quick lookups, AI Studio for raw model access, Julius for data, and OpenRouter when I want to compare models without switching tabs. That's it.

Open a tab. Try one. Right now. You'll thank yourself later.
